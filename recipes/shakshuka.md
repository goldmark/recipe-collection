# Shakshuka

**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Cook time:** 20 min
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 400g canned diced tomatoes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 large eggs
- Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell
    pepper, and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for
    another minute until fragrant. Stir in the cumin, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper (if using) and cook for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
2. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer the sauce for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens slightly.
3. Make four small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each well. Cover the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes, or until the egg whites are set but the yolks are still runny (or cook longer if you prefer firmer yolks).
4. Garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro and serve immediately with crusty bread for dipping.

## Notes

- You can customize the sauce by adding ingredients like feta cheese, olives, or spinach.
- For a spicier version, add more cayenne pepper or a dash of hot sauce.
- Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheated gently on the stovetop or in the microwave.

## Variations

- Shakshuka with sausage: Add cooked chorizo or merguez sausage to the sauce for extra flavor and protein.
- Green shakshuka: Use green vegetables like spinach, kale, and green bell peppers instead of tomatoes for a fresh twist on the classic recipe.
- Shakshuka with feta: Crumble feta cheese over the top of the sauce before adding the eggs for a tangy, creamy addition.
