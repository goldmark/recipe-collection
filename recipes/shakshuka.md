# Shakshuka

**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Cook time:** 20 min
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 400g canned diced tomatoes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 large eggs
- Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add onion and bell pepper; sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Stir in cumin, smoked paprika, and cayenne; cook 30 seconds to toast the spices.
5. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices and season with salt and pepper.
6. Simmer the sauce for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
7. Make four small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each.
8. Cover the skillet and cook for 5–7 minutes, until whites are set but yolks are still runny.
9. Garnish with parsley or cilantro and serve immediately with crusty bread.

## Notes

- Good base for customizing — I've thrown in feta, olives, and spinach at various points. All work.
- More cayenne or a dash of hot sauce takes it up nicely if you want heat.
- Leftovers keep fine in the fridge for 2 days; reheat gently on the stovetop.

## Variations

- Shakshuka with sausage: Add cooked chorizo or merguez sausage to the sauce for extra flavor and protein.
- Green shakshuka: Use green vegetables like spinach, kale, and green bell peppers instead of tomatoes for a fresh twist on the classic recipe.
- Shakshuka with feta: Crumble feta cheese over the top of the sauce before adding the eggs for a tangy, creamy addition.
