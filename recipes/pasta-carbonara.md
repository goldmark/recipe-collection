# Pasta Carbonara

**Cuisine:** Italian
**Servings:** 2
**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 15 min
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 100g guanciale (pancetta works in a pinch)
- 2 whole eggs + 2 egg yolks
- 50g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
- Black pepper, freshly cracked
- Salt for the pasta water

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti.
2. While the pasta cooks, dice the guanciale and render it in a cold pan over medium heat until crisp. Turn off the heat.
3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, yolks, Pecorino, and a generous amount of black pepper into a paste.
4. When the pasta is al dente, reserve a mug of pasta water and drain.
5. Add the hot pasta to the pan with the guanciale. Toss to coat in the rendered fat.
6. Off the heat, pour in the egg mixture and toss vigorously, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce turns silky. Do not return to heat or the eggs will scramble.
7. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and pepper on top.

## Notes

- The pan must be off the heat when the eggs go in. The residual warmth of the pasta is enough.
- Guanciale really does taste different from pancetta — worth seeking out.
- Skip the cream. Always.

## Variations

- Cacio e pepe-leaning version: skip the guanciale, double the pepper.
- Add a handful of peas at the end for color (controversial but tasty).
