# Bread Pudding

**Cuisine:** British
**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 15 min
**Cook time:** 45 min
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

- 4 cups of cubed bread (preferably stale)
- 2 cups of milk
- 1/2 cup of sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon of ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon of salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the milk, sugar, eggs, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt until well combined.
3. Add the cubed bread to the bowl and gently toss to coat with the milk mixture.
4. Let it sit for about 10 minutes to allow the bread to soak up the liquid.
5. Pour the bread mixture into a greased baking dish and spread it out evenly.
6. Bake in the preheated oven for 40-45 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the pudding is set.
7. Remove from the oven and let it cool slightly before serving.

## Notes

- The soak time really matters — I skipped it once and the bread stayed dry in the middle. 10 minutes minimum.
- Stale bread works noticeably better than fresh; fresh bread turns mushy rather than custardy.
- I'd add raisins next time — felt like something was missing without them.

## Variations

- For a richer pudding, substitute half of the milk with heavy cream.
- For a dairy-free version, use almond milk or coconut milk instead of regular milk.
- For a spiced version, add a pinch of nutmeg or cloves along with the cinnamon.
- For a fruity twist, mix in chopped apples or pears before baking.
- For a boozy version, add a splash of rum or bourbon to the milk mixture for added depth of flavor.
- For a chocolate version, mix in cocoa powder to the milk mixture and add chocolate chips to the bread mixture before baking.
- For a savory version, omit the sugar and cinnamon, and add herbs, cheese, and cooked vegetables to the bread mixture before baking.
