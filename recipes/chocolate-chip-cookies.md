# Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Cuisine:** Dessert
**Servings:** ~18 cookies
**Prep time:** 15 min
**Cook time:** 12 min
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

- 225g unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 200g brown sugar
- 100g white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 320g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 300g chocolate chips (or chopped dark chocolate)
- Flaky sea salt, to finish

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180°C / 350°F. Line two trays with parchment.
2. Whisk melted butter with both sugars until smooth.
3. Whisk in eggs and vanilla.
4. In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt.
5. Fold dry ingredients into wet until just combined. Stir in chocolate.
6. Scoop into balls (about 2 tbsp each) and place on trays with space between.
7. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until edges are golden but centers look underdone.
8. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes — they finish setting as they cool.
9. Sprinkle with flaky salt while warm.

## Notes

- Pulling them out underdone is the secret. They keep cooking on the hot tray.
- Brown butter instead of melted butter takes them up a notch.
- Chilling the dough overnight genuinely improves the flavor.

## Variations

- Add 100g chopped pecans or walnuts.
- Stuff with a square of caramel for molten centers.
- Replace half the chocolate with white chocolate and dried cranberries.
