# Chicken Tikka Masala

**Cuisine:** Indian (British-Indian, technically)
**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 15 min (plus 1 hr marinating)
**Cook time:** 30 min
**Difficulty:** Medium

## Ingredients

### For the chicken
- 600g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 200g plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- Salt

### For the sauce
- 2 tbsp butter or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp paprika
- 400g tinned tomatoes (or passata)
- 200ml double cream
- Fresh coriander, to serve

## Instructions

1. Mix all the marinade ingredients with the chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, ideally overnight.
2. Grill or pan-sear the chicken until charred on the edges and just cooked through. Set aside.
3. In a large pan, melt the butter and cook the onion until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.
4. Add garlic, ginger, and dry spices. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add tomatoes, simmer for 10 minutes, then blend smooth with a stick blender.
6. Stir in cream and the cooked chicken. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.
7. Serve with rice or naan, topped with fresh coriander.

## Notes

- Thighs > breasts for this dish. They stay tender.
- The char on the chicken matters — don't skip the sear step.
- Make a double batch of sauce and freeze half.

## Variations

- Swap chicken for paneer or roasted cauliflower.
- Add a teaspoon of kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) at the end for a more authentic flavor.
